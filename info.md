# Chime Guard: Silent Google Cast Playback

Chime Guard automatically eliminates the loud, unconfigurable connection chime ("beep") played by Google Home and Nest speakers when starting playback or switching Cast receiver apps.

### Key Highlights:
- **Zero Audible Chimes**: Completely swallows cold-start connection chimes.
- **External Cast Takeover Protection**: Automatically silences receiver-switch chimes when taking over an active mobile Spotify or YouTube stream.
- **Instant Track Skips**: Preserves instantaneous track changes and pause/resumes during active Music Assistant sessions.
- **Universal Dashboard Interception**: Protects custom cards, media players, and dashboard clicks automatically without modifying YAML.
- **Automation Support**: Includes `chime_guard.silent_play` service for automations, scripts, and voice assistants.
